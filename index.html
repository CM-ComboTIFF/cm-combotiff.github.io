CM ComboTIFF

Batch imaging for Mac. Merge images into multi-page TIFF files or extract pages into individual TIFF, JPEG, or PNG. Effortless document management with resize and compression options.

Seamless Merging

Combine TIFF, JPG, and PNG files into a single multi-page TIFF. Ideal for archiving business documents in one organized file—no more scattered folders.

Precise Extraction

Split multi-page TIFFs into single-page TIFF, JPG, or PNG images. Retain quality while converting formats for any workflow.

Smart Resizing

Resize by ratio, percentage, or pixels. Scale images uniformly across batches without manual adjustments.

Advanced Compression

Apply TIFF compression: JPEG, LZW, Packbits, CCITT G3, or G4. Optimize file sizes while preserving clarity for storage and sharing.

How It Works

1. Add Files
Drag and drop TIFF, JPG, or PNG images into the app. Supports batch selection for efficiency.

2. Choose Mode
Select merge to create multi-page TIFF or extract to split into individual files. Set resize and compression options.

3. Process Batch
Review settings and run. The app handles everything in parallel, delivering results instantly.

4. Export
Save optimized files to your preferred location. Ready for archiving, sharing, or further use.
